How an Ember is forged

Buy, record, mint, close

Embers can only be forged while $ROCK is on the curve. Once it graduates at a $200,000 market cap, the collection is limited forever.

  1. Buy on the curve

    Buy any amount of $ROCK while it trades on the Meteora bonding curve.

  2. The hook records it

    A transfer hook runs on every trade and writes your buy on-chain: wallet, size and order. Nobody can edit it.

  3. Your Ember is minted

    Claim it yourself or let the forge mint it for you. Its flame matches the size of that buy.

  4. Graduation closes the forge

    At a $200,000 market cap the curve fills and $ROCK moves to a Meteora pool with locked liquidity. The hook switches off and no Ember can be forged again.

Sell during the curve and your Ember burns out

The hook marks every wallet that sells before graduation. Their Embers turn to ash for good. Hold until graduation and your Ember keeps its flame.

Questions

Before you buy

Why only one token?

One is the smallest whole supply a token can have. With nine decimals it still splits into a billion units, so even a tiny buy gets you thousands of units.

What happens after graduation?

Graduation happens when $ROCK reaches a $200,000 market cap. It keeps trading on a Meteora pool whose liquidity is locked forever. The transfer hook is removed at graduation, so trades after that forge nothing. The Embers collection is closed for good.

Can I game the rarity?

The tier depends only on the size of a single buy, and every buy is public on-chain. Splitting a buy gives you more Embers at a cooler tier. The three Supernovas go to events that can each happen only once.

Where will I see my Ember?

In any Solana wallet or marketplace that shows NFTs, such as Phantom. Embers are Metaplex Core NFTs.
